CHARGE FRONT
============
First-person tactical shooter — recover a lost clone-DNA vial-pendrive from an
abandoned facility on a purple-spotted infested planet. Advance a 4-man squad,
power 3 relays (each power-up draws the horde — hold the ground), then extract.

Built with:
  - LittleJS (MIT) — tiny HTML5 game engine by Frank Force
    https://github.com/KilledByAPixel/LittleJS
  - Three.js (MIT) — 3D library
    https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

Sound effects:
  - Extracted from Soundly (https://soundly.com) — shipped in the SFX/ folder:
      * LaserGun0-3.wav, LaserGunSparks0-1.wav  (player/squad laser + impact sparks)
      * LaserGunReload0-2.wav, LaserGunEmpty.wav (weapon reload / dry fire)
      * UnitHit0-2.wav, UnitArmorHit.wav         (player hurt / squad armor hit)
      * AlienCreatureDead0-1.wav                 (enemy death)
      * AlienCreatureNormal0-1.wav, AlienCreatureRoar.wav (creature aggro / boss roar)
      * Unburrow0-1.wav                          (creature emerging from the ground)
      * FootstepPebble0-4.wav, FootstepMetal0-4.wav (footsteps — wastes vs. facility)
      * GrenadeExplosion.wav                     (grenade blast)
      * AccessValid.wav, AccessInvalid.wav       (crafting / UI confirm & deny)
      * DroneHit0-2.wav                          (facility security drone hits)
      * SpaceshipLandingLoop.wav, SpaceshipLanded.wav (intro landing cutscene)
    Originals (24-bit) are kept in SFX/original/; the playable copies are 16-bit.
  - Remaining game sounds (screech, spit, relay hum/power, pickup blip, jump,
    throw, revive, command, vial, ambient wind) are synthesized in-browser via
    LittleJS's bundled ZzFX generator.

Open chargefront.html in a browser to play.
Tip: open with ?preview=1 for a cinematic flyover of the terrain.
